## Description

Stop making clients search through email for project updates, files, approvals, and invoices.

I will configure ClientDesk as a professional white-label portal for your agency, consultancy, or service business. Your team gets a focused studio dashboard, and every client gets a private project link.

Features can include:

- Your business branding and welcome copy
- Separate private client workspaces
- Project status, progress, plan, and target date
- Milestone roadmap
- Client-facing updates
- Deliverable and preview links
- Approval and change-request workflow
- Invoice status and optional payment links
- Responsive desktop and mobile experience
- Railway deployment and handoff documentation

This is a focused productized setup, not an unlimited custom software project. Message me with your workflow before ordering if you need a custom integration.

## FAQ

### Is hosting included?

Configuration is included according to the package. Recurring hosting, domain, storage, and payment-provider charges remain in your accounts.

### Can I resell the code?

No. White-label use for your own client work is permitted according to the purchased license. Resale and redistribution rights require a separate agreement.

### Is this suitable for sensitive records?

The standard product is not designed for medical, government identity, payment-card, or other regulated records.
