# Contra service

## Service title

White-label client portal for agencies and consultants

## One-line description

A branded client workspace for onboarding, project progress, deliverables, approvals, and invoice visibility.

## Overview

ClientDesk replaces scattered delivery emails and file links with one calm, professional client experience.

I configure the portal around your brand and workflow. Your team receives a private studio dashboard to manage client projects, while each customer receives a dedicated portal link showing their progress, milestones, updates, deliverables, approval requests, and invoice status.

## Best for

- Creative and development agencies
- Consultants and coaches
- Productized service businesses
- Independent studios
- Client operations teams replacing spreadsheets

## Deliverables

- White-label business setup
- Responsive product and client-portal interface
- Separate client workspace links
- Progress and milestone workflow
- Deliverable library
- Approval and change-request workflow
- Invoice-status display and optional payment links
- Protected studio dashboard
- Deployment and handoff documentation

## Starting price

$39 for a defined branded setup. Managed deployment and source-code handoff are available in higher packages.

## Timeline

Three to seven days after complete buyer requirements, depending on the selected package.

## Boundaries

ClientDesk uses external links for deliverables and payments. It does not provide encrypted file storage or payment processing. It is a focused single-business deployment and is not intended for regulated data without a separately reviewed architecture.
