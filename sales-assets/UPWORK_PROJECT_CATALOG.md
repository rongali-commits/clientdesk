## FAQ

### Can I use my own branding?

Yes. Business name, logo letters, primary color, accent color, welcome copy, and support email are configurable.

### Does every client get a separate portal?

Yes. Every workspace receives a unique private link and shows only that client's project information.

### Can clients approve work?

Yes. Clients can approve a defined request or ask for changes with an optional note.

### Does it accept payments?

ClientDesk tracks invoice status and can link to your buyer-owned payment page. It does not process or store payment-card data.

### Can I upload files?

The product organizes links to files, documents, previews, and videos stored in services you already own. Secure file storage is outside the standard package.
