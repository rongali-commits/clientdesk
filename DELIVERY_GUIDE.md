# ClientDesk delivery guide

## Deliver to the buyer

- Public product URL
- Studio dashboard URL
- First private client-portal URL
- Secure admin-token handoff method
- Buyer-specific scope summary
- Hosting and database ownership details
- Backup and restore responsibility
- Included revision and support dates

## Acceptance checks

Ask the buyer to confirm:

1. Branding and contact information are correct.
2. The private client portal opens only with its unique link.
3. Progress, dates, milestones, updates, and deliverables are correct.
4. Approval decisions work as expected.
5. Invoice status and payment links are correct.
6. The experience works on desktop and mobile.
7. The studio dashboard is inaccessible without the private token.

## Ongoing operation

- Create one portal per client.
- Share the private portal URL only with that client.
- Post short project updates consistently.
- Use approvals for a specific decision, not general conversation.
- Keep external deliverable permissions current.
- Update invoice status after payment is confirmed by the actual payment provider.
- Back up the persistent database regularly.

## Not included unless stated

- Domain registration and recurring hosting fees
- Secure file-storage infrastructure
- Payment processing
- Email or chat notifications
- Full user accounts, password reset, or social login
- Multi-business SaaS tenancy
- Regulated-data compliance
- Ongoing maintenance
- Resale or redistribution rights
