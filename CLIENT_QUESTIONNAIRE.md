# ClientDesk buyer questionnaire

## Business and branding

1. Legal or public business name
2. Short display name
3. Logo or one to three logo letters
4. Primary and accent colors in hex format
5. Support email shown to clients
6. Approved welcome message
7. Privacy-policy URL, if applicable

## Client workflow

1. What services or projects will use the portal?
2. How many client workspaces should be configured at delivery?
3. Which project statuses do you use?
4. What milestone stages should appear in the first workspace?
5. What decisions normally require client approval?
6. Which deliverable links should be added?
7. Which invoice references and statuses should be shown?

## Deployment

1. Buyer-owned Railway or compatible hosting account
2. Preferred subdomain, such as `clients.example.com`
3. Person authorized to manage DNS
4. Required delivery date
5. Whether source-code handoff is included in the purchased package

## Safety confirmation

- Do not send passwords, API keys, OTPs, payment-card data, or sensitive customer records in marketplace chat.
- Use account invitations and server environment variables for required access.
- Confirm that private portal links will be shared only with their intended clients.
- Confirm that the portal will not be used for regulated health, financial, identity, or legal records without a separately reviewed architecture.
